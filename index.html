Manifesto

We exist because critical decisions are still made in the dark.

Data arrives late, scattered and without context. And those who protect people and cities have to act now.

OPRO brings together what is scattered, shows what matters and puts control back in the hands of whoever answers for the decision.

Technology in service of those who serve.

Database + AI

A living database.
An intelligence that acts.

Every OPRO operation runs on a single database connected to artificial intelligence models. Each signal that arrives, from a radar, a satellite or a sensor, is stored, matched against history and turned into a decision in seconds.

  1. Decision

    Alerts, maps and actions for teams in the field.

  2. AI models

    Detect patterns, risks and change.

  3. Database

    Every reading stored, dated and verifiable.

  4. Ingestion

    Real time streams, cleaned and unified.

  5. Sources

    Radars, Satellites, Sensors, Cameras, Drones

Technology

Precision where it matters.

Every signal arrives with a coordinate, a timestamp and a confidence level. OPRO cross checks different sources on the same point to confirm where something is, not just estimate it.

Principles

What we do not negotiate.

  1. Humans decide. AI supports.

  2. Data belongs to the client.

    It stays under the custody and rules of whoever produced it.

  3. Every action is traceable.
